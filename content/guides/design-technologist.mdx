# What is a design technologist?

A design technologist is a designer who builds in code. The role sits inside a design team and makes things people can use before engineering commits to them: interactive prototypes, design-system components, and small tools that speed the team up.

The title often covers the same person as design engineer. The difference is usually where the work lands: a prototype that settles a decision, or the production interface itself. The same words are also used in building design, where the job means CAD and BIM work, and search results mix the two. This guide is about the software role: the person between product design and frontend engineering, and the nearby titles teams post for it.

## What does a design technologist do?

A design technologist builds working prototypes, design-system code, and internal tools so a team can test an idea before it is expensive to change. The prototypes use real interactions and often real data. They then help engineering carry the decision into production, or hand the validated version to the people who ship it.

Companies that use the title describe it in similar terms. [Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/how-we-build/design-technologists-the-role-that-turns-creativity-into-code) says people in the role build “real, interactive, data-connected prototypes,” guide tokens, and share scripts and extensions that translate design intent into code for engineering. A 2019 [Indeed Design article](https://indeed.design/article/what-is-a-design-technologist/) lists three kinds of work: prototypes for experiments, support for the design system, and tools that help teams build and make decisions. At Indeed, a validated solution then goes to UX developers for delivery.

The prototype is the center of the job. A design file shows a state. A coded prototype shows what happens between states: long names, slow responses, empty lists. Atlassian’s phrasing for the goal is moving a team from “I think this will work” to “I know this is right” before it is expensive to find out. The same post reports more than 12,000 AI-powered prototypes running across Atlassian teams, and describes the role building AI into design-system scaffolding so other designers can make coded prototypes themselves. Part of the role is now making prototyping cheap for everyone else, not only building each prototype by hand.

## Is a design technologist the same as a design engineer?

Often, yes. Atlassian names design engineer and creative technologist as other names for the role, and Indeed’s design technologists sit on a team called Design Engineering. Where the titles split, technologist postings lean toward prototypes and tooling inside a design org, and design engineer postings lean toward production UI the company ships.

Atlassian’s short version is “think like a designer, but code like an engineer.” The [what is a design engineer](/guides/what-is-a-design-engineer) guide describes the design engineer the same way, with one emphasis: the prototype and the production UI are both the main output, in the codebase the company ships. A posting under the technologist title is more likely to stop at a prototype that engineering adopts.

That is a tendency, not a rule. Mithrl, a drug-discovery startup, hires for the role inside engineering under the title Member of Technical Staff, Design Technology. The posting asks the person to own complex frontend surfaces and says the prototypes “need to hold up under production conditions.” That seat is a design engineer in everything but the noun.

The quickest test is one sentence in the posting: does the person write production code, or stop at the prototype? The [design engineer job description](/guides/design-engineer-job-description) guide asks hiring teams to say this plainly, because candidates read the title and assume.

## How does a design technologist compare with nearby titles?

The titles overlap more than they differ. UX engineer is the title Google uses for designer-engineers who prototype and also write production UI code. Creative technologist often points to brand, campaign, or agency work. Prototyper names the output and nothing else. Read the responsibilities first, because the same seat is posted under all of these titles.

**UX engineer.** Google’s [UX Engineering](https://uxe.withgoogle.com/) page lists a typical day that includes writing and reviewing production code for UI features, coding prototypes for design projects, and working with UX researchers on what a study prototype needs. That is the same job with a larger share of production code. See [UX engineer jobs](/roles/ux-engineer). The [UX engineer guide](/guides/ux-engineer) covers the role in full.

**Creative technologist.** Advertising teams use either title. Amazon Ads calls the people in its Brand Innovation Lab design technologists, and its head of North America design technology describes them brainstorming with creatives and leading the technical side of campaigns. He holds a master’s degree in creative technology. See [creative technologist jobs](/roles/creative-technologist).

**Prototyper.** The narrowest title. It names the deliverable and says nothing about production. See [prototyper jobs](/roles/prototyper).

When a posting is mostly tokens and components shared across products, the title is often design systems engineer. Those roles are on [design systems engineer jobs](/roles/design-systems-engineer).

## What skills do design technologist postings ask for?

Design technologist postings ask for front-end code, a design portfolio, and prototyping experience, usually all three. They want code samples next to the portfolio, years in front-end or UX prototyping work, and comfort inside a design system. Recent postings add AI: prototypes of AI-driven workflows, generative tooling, or AI in the design-to-code pipeline.

Two postings and one team article, all checked on 20 October 2026, show the range:

- **Amazon, Design Technologist, eShop** (Gurugram, India). Asks for 3+ years of experience in “front-end technology, engineering, or UX prototyping,” coding samples in front-end languages, and an online portfolio. The work is generative AI pipelines for product images and video.
- **Mithrl, Member of Technical Staff, Design Technology** (San Francisco, on-site). Asks for a “frontend expert (React, TypeScript, CSS)” with 5+ years in product design or frontend engineering, and a focus on design technology or prototyping.
- **Indeed Design’s description of the tool kit.** Accessibility standards, responsive web design, site performance, internationalization and localization, native app development, and modern front-end frameworks, plus a feel for interaction design.

The Amazon Ads interview says most people in design technology do not have a formal degree in the field. Many come from computer science, teach themselves, or start through coding boot camps. The portfolio carries the application, and it needs something that runs. The [design engineer portfolio checklist](/tools/design-engineer-portfolio-checklist) covers what reviewers look for.

The skills are not unique to the title. On this board, 26 of the 37 open listings on 20 October 2026 mention prototyping in the description, and 13 mention both prototyping and Figma. None of them carries the technologist title. [Figma design engineer jobs](/skills/figma) and [design system jobs](/skills/design-systems) are where the same skills show up under other titles.

## How many design technologist jobs are open, and what do they pay?

Few, on this board. On 20 October 2026, none of its 37 open software listings had design technologist in the title, so there is no posted pay to report for the title. The salary pages that rank for the query include hourly figures and a design technician page, which describe different jobs.

The board’s own counts on that date, from 37 open listings at 25 companies: 30 filed as design engineer, 4 as design systems engineer, 1 as creative technologist (OpusClip, on-site in Mountain View), 1 as UX engineer (Reddit, Principal UX Engineer, Ads, remote in the US), and 1 as UI engineer. No title contained prototyper.

The title is posted elsewhere. On the same date, Amazon’s eShop role and Mithrl’s design technology role were open. So was a Senior Design Technologist role at ICON in Austin, which is the building-design sense: Revit and BIM/CAD tooling for construction. A software board would not file it, and a candidate searching the title will find both kinds of posting.
