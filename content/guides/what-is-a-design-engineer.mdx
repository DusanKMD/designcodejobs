# What is a design engineer?

A software design engineer builds the interface, not the enclosure. They sit between product design and frontend engineering: they prototype in code, shape design systems, and ship UI that matches the craft of the design. This is the same question as “what do design engineers do,” answered for software. Mechanical, industrial, and chip design engineers are a different job that happens to share the title.

This page is an outline of the full guide. The sections below are the argument, not the finished essay.

## Outline

### The software meaning, stated in one paragraph
- Define the role with the vocabulary of the field: Figma, React, prototypes, tokens, motion, accessibility.
- Say explicitly what it is not: CAD, mechanical design, physical design, AV.
- Point at the open list of [design engineer jobs](/).

### What do design engineers do day to day
- Turn a designed flow into a working prototype, often the same day.
- Build and maintain the components other designers and engineers reuse.
- Pair with product designers on interaction details and with frontend engineers on production constraints.
- Write, review, and sometimes own the CSS and component API.

### Three archetypes
- Product: in-app UI, design systems, quality of the product surface.
- Brand and web: marketing sites, campaigns, Framer or custom web work. See [Framer jobs](/skills/framer).
- Systems: tokens, documentation, multi-platform components. See [design systems engineer jobs](/roles/design-systems-engineer).

### How this differs from a frontend engineer and a product designer
- Frontend engineer: broader platform ownership, less of the visual and interaction call.
- Product designer: owns the problem and the design, less of the production code.
- The overlap is the job. A later article will put the comparison in a table.
