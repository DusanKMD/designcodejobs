# Design engineer salary guide

Search results for “design engineer salary” are dominated by mechanical and manufacturing pay. The software number is a different dataset. Live ranges from postings on this board are on the [design engineer salary](/salary) page. This guide is the outline for the write-up around that data.

## Outline

### What the number is, and what it is not
- Employer-posted base salary only. No Glassdoor estimates, no total-comp guesses, no equity counted as cash.
- Say when a range is yearly, and drop hourly or OTE figures from the headline.
- Note the sample size next to every figure so a single posting cannot look like a market.

### Design engineer salary by level
- Internship and junior, senior, staff, lead.
- Use the same seniority rules as the job list.
- Link [design engineer internship](/seniority/internship) for the entry-level path. Senior and junior pages stay thin until volume shows up.

### Breakouts that have their own pages
- [UX engineer salary](/salary/ux-engineer) — the highest-CPC salary query in this niche.
- [UI engineer salary](/salary/ui-engineer).
- [Design technologist salary](/salary/design-technologist).
- [Design systems engineer salary](/salary/design-systems-engineer).
- [Creative technologist salary](/salary/creative-technologist).

### Remote versus office
- Compare fully remote postings with city-specific ones only when both have at least a few ranges.
- Do not invent a city premium. City pages are not a priority; US search volume for “design engineer jobs nyc” and similar is small next to the role and salary terms.

### How to read a posted range
- A range is the employer’s band for the requisition, not an offer.
- Level inside one req (IC3 vs IC5) can explain a wide band.
- Currency stays as posted. No silent USD conversion.

### What gets updated monthly
- Median midpoint, count of postings with pay, and which companies published.
- The [salary page](/salary) is the dataset. This article explains the method and links every breakout.
